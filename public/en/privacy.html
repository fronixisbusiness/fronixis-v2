PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Fronixis respects your privacy. This page explains how personal data and technical information may be processed when you use our website.

PRINCIPLE

Data minimisation

We aim to process only the information that is necessary to operate, secure and improve the Fronixis website and to provide its content and services.

CONTROL

Your choices

Where consent is required, optional analytics, marketing and affiliate technologies should only be used after you have made the corresponding choice in our cookie settings.

TRANSPARENCY

Clear information

We aim to explain the technologies and third-party services used by Fronixis transparently and update this policy when the website or its services change.

WEBSITE OPERATION

Technical data processing.

When a website is accessed, technical information may be processed automatically in order to deliver and protect the site.

ACCESS DATA

Server and connection information

Technical systems may process information such as IP addresses, browser type, operating system, requested pages, timestamps and other connection data required to provide and secure the website.

SECURITY

Protection of the website

Technical information may also be processed to detect abuse, maintain website security, investigate technical errors and protect Fronixis against malicious traffic.

HOSTING

Hosting infrastructure

Fronixis may use external hosting, delivery or infrastructure providers to make the website available. Such providers may process technical data as part of providing their services.

COOKIES & TECHNOLOGIES

Necessary and optional technologies.

Fronixis distinguishes between technologies that are necessary for the operation of the website and optional technologies used for analytics, marketing or affiliate purposes.

ESSENTIAL

Technically necessary

Necessary technologies may be used to provide core website functionality, store privacy preferences, protect the site and maintain essential technical functions.

ANALYTICS

Analytics technologies

If analytics tools are activated in the future, they may help us understand how visitors use Fronixis. Where consent is legally required, such technologies should only be activated after consent has been provided.

MARKETING

Marketing and affiliate technologies

Affiliate networks and advertising partners may use tracking technologies to determine whether a visit, registration or purchase originated from a Fronixis link. Where required, these technologies are subject to user consent.

AFFILIATE MARKETING

Affiliate links and conversion tracking.

Fronixis may participate in affiliate programs and may receive commissions when users complete qualifying actions after following an affiliate link.

TRACKING

Attribution

Affiliate networks may use cookies, tracking parameters or similar technologies to attribute a transaction or registration to Fronixis.

THIRD PARTIES

Partner privacy policies

When you follow a link to an external merchant or service, the privacy practices and terms of that third party apply to your use of the external website.

DISCLOSURE

Transparency

Additional information about the commercial nature of affiliate links can be found in our Affiliate Disclosure.

COMMUNICATION

Contact and newsletter data.

Additional personal data may be processed when you actively provide information to Fronixis.

CONTACT

Messages and inquiries

If you contact Fronixis in the future by email or through a contact form, the information you provide may be processed in order to respond to and manage your inquiry.

NEWSLETTER

Newsletter subscriptions

If Fronixis offers a newsletter, subscription information such as an email address may be processed to provide the requested communication. Specific information about the newsletter provider and procedures will be added when the service becomes active.

WITHDRAWAL

Consent can be withdrawn

Where processing is based on consent, you can generally withdraw that consent for future processing. Cookie preferences can be changed using the Fronixis cookie settings.

YOUR RIGHTS

Data protection rights.

Depending on the applicable data protection law and the circumstances of the processing, individuals may have various rights regarding their personal data.

ACCESS

Access and information

You may have the right to request information about personal data processed about you and to receive access to that information where applicable.

CONTROL

Correction and deletion

Depending on the legal requirements, you may have the right to request correction, deletion, restriction of processing or object to certain forms of processing.

COMPLAINTS

Supervisory authorities

Where applicable, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

POLICY UPDATES

Fronixis will evolve. This policy will too.

This privacy policy may be updated when new services, analytics tools, affiliate networks, newsletter systems or other technologies are introduced.
